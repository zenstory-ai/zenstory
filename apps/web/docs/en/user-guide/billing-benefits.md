# Plans and benefits

Open **Plans & Benefits** in the top dashboard navigation (or click the **Manage Subscription** icon in the project header) to check your current plan, today's AI messages, active projects, owned custom skills, monthly reference novel breakdowns, and Pro expiration date. You can also view the public [pricing page](https://app.zenstory.ai/pricing) without signing in.

## Free vs. Pro comparison

| Benefit | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily AI messages** | **3 per day** | **Unlimited** |
| **Active projects** | **3** | **Unlimited** |
| **Owned custom skills** | **3** | **20** |
| **Reference novel breakdowns** | Not included in monthly plan (**1 one-time 3-chapter trial** per account) | **5 per month** (up to 300,000 characters per book) |
| **Manual file version history** | **10 per file** | **100 per file** |
| **TXT manuscript export & file download** | Included | Included |

**Quota and reset rules**:
- **Daily AI messages**: Free accounts reset to 3 messages at **00:00 Beijing time (UTC+8)** each day (free accounts on the same network also share a daily free network allowance). When the system determines that a turn produced no substantive output and refunds the message, the interface notes that the turn was not counted toward today's allowance. When your daily messages are used up, save your current draft and either continue after 00:00 Beijing time or upgrade to Pro.
- **Reference novel breakdowns**: Pro's 5 monthly breakdowns reset at **00:00 Beijing time on the 1st of each month**. Free accounts get 1 one-time trial (**Try the first 3 chapters free**, up to 15,000 characters), which is separate from the Pro monthly allowance.
- **Projects and custom skills**: Counted by the active projects and custom skills you currently own; deleting an unused project or custom skill frees a slot.

## Compare plans

On the public [pricing page](https://app.zenstory.ai/pricing) or **Plans & Benefits**, switch between **Monthly** and **Yearly** billing. On the pricing page, the **Show differences only** toggle hides rows that are identical across plans; if every displayed row already differs between Free and Pro, toggling it leaves the table unchanged. Current prices, discounts, and checkout totals are shown on the page and order screen.

## Upgrade, renew, or redeem a code

- **Activate or renew Pro**: Choose a monthly (30-day) or yearly (365-day) duration and complete the one-time payment—subscriptions **do not auto-renew**. Renewing while Pro is active extends your current expiration date. After returning from checkout, verify your plan status and expiration date; if payment confirmation is still processing, wait rather than paying again. If you were charged without receiving Pro benefits, keep your order details and contact [support@zenstory.ai](mailto:support@zenstory.ai).
- **Redeem a code**: To use a Pro redemption code, click the gear icon in the top right to open **Settings → Subscription**, click **Redeem Code**, enter your code, and confirm.

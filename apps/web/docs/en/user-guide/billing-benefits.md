# Plans and benefits

Open **Plans & Benefits** in the workbench to check your plan, today's messages, projects, owned skills, and Pro expiration. The public [pricing page](https://app.zenstory.ai/pricing) is also available without login.

## Free and Pro

| Benefit | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI messages | 3 per day | Unlimited |
| Projects | 3 | Unlimited |
| Owned custom skills | 3 | 20 |
| Material breakdowns | Not included | 5 per month |
| Manuscript export | TXT | TXT |

Free messages reset at 00:00 Beijing time (UTC+8). Monthly material allowance resets on the first day of the month. Custom skills count those you currently own; deleting an unused owned skill frees a slot.

When the free-trial entry is available, accounts without Materials access can try the first chapters of one book. This is separate from the Pro monthly allowance; check the entry for the trial limits.

When today's messages are used up, save your idea and draft. Continue after the reset or get Pro.

## Compare plans

Switch between monthly and yearly pricing. **Show differences only** hides shared benefits. If every displayed benefit already differs, toggling it leaves the same content visible.

Check the page and order for current prices, discounts, and payment amount.

## Activate or renew

Choose a duration and follow the Alipay payment steps. Payments are one-time and do not renew automatically. Monthly benefits last 30 days, yearly benefits 365 days; renewing extends expiration.

After returning, check your plan and expiration. If processing is pending, wait rather than paying again. For a charge without benefits, retain the order information and contact support.

Redeem a code on Plans & Benefits when you have one.

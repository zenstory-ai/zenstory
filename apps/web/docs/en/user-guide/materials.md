# Materials Library

The library breaks reference novels into chapter summaries, characters, lore, and other items for reading and writing. Uploading a reference is different from importing your own draft.

## Free trial and Pro

Materials Library breakdowns are a Pro benefit. When the page offers a free trial, free accounts can try the opening chapters of one book within the entry's stated limits. Check the page for available attempts and chapter scope. When selecting a full book, the interface identifies the chapters retained for analysis. Trial results remain available to read.

Pro includes 5 breakdowns per month, with up to 300,000 characters per book. Check the library and Plans & Benefits for current availability. If a platform failure occurs, inspect the refund message before uploading again.

## Prepare and upload

1. Open Materials in the workbench and choose Upload (Pro), or the free trial when the page offers one.
2. Select a TXT you have the right to use; check its title and chapter scope.
3. Confirm and wait for processing.

Files must be at most 20MB with recognizable headings such as “第一章,” “第一回,” or “Chapter 1.” Pro checks the 300,000-character limit across the book; the trial checks the chapters retained within the stated trial scope. This entry does not accept PDF, Word, or EPUB.

## Read the results

Upload success still requires analysis time. Open material details, expand chapters, characters, or lore, and select an item. Categories vary by material and task; an empty category does not prove the source lacks that content.

Summaries help locate evidence; source text helps verify it. Return to the passage when an interpretation is unclear or incomplete, and record your own reading separately.

For a failed task, inspect its reason and quota status. Follow Retry when offered; retrying trial material may require Pro, and a retry creates a new task.

## Reference it in a project

Open a project's Materials tab, select relevant items, and attach them to chat. You can also quote a source passage. State the question:

```text
Use the attached passage to analyze the action where the character changes their goal.
Separate source facts from interpretation. Do not continue my story yet.
```

Attachments remain subject to a context budget; the full library is not injected every turn. See [material analysis](../advanced/material-analysis.md) for an example.

Import your own manuscript through the project's draft upload control. See [file management](file-tree.md).

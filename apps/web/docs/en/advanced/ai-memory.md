# AI Memory and context

AI Memory is a short project summary for background, style, and progress. Keep detailed characters, outlines, and prose in files, and identify what each task needs.

## Read and edit memory

Open **AI Memory** at the top of project chat, edit, and save. Cancel does not submit changes. Reopen to check important corrections.

| Field | Useful contents | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project Summary | Genre, central situation, viewpoint | 4,000 characters |
| Writing Style | Expression preferences and approaches to avoid | 1,000 characters |
| Current Phase | What is saved and the next task | 1,000 characters |
| Notes | Easily confused facts, open decisions, relevant filenames | 4,000 characters |

You do not need to fill the limits. Put important constraints first and detailed background in files. The AI can update the summary, writing style, and current phase; check stored results after important decisions. Notes are yours: when you explicitly say "remember ..." in chat, the AI appends your words to notes, and the result card in the chat lists what it saved with an **Undo** button. Otherwise the AI does not write notes and keeps its suggestions in its replies.

## Separate decisions and progress

Separate author knowledge, character knowledge, and events already written. After Scene 1 of Before Closing reaches the mismatched item:

```text
Summary: Realist mystery in Lin He's limited viewpoint.
Style: Advance through questions and observable actions.
Current Phase: Scene 1 saved; the bag remains at the desk. Scene 2 is unwritten.
Notes: Lin does not know why the description is wrong. Shen's relationship
with the owner and the final recipient remain undecided.
Evidence: Lin He, Shen Min, Three-scene outline, Scene 1 — The claim.
```

Do not turn suspicion into an exposed fraud or record the next scene's plan as completed.

## Correct a mistaken memory

Compare prose and character notes, find the outdated field, and change that part:

```text
Check AI Memory against Scene 1 and the character files.
List conflicting sentences, evidence, and replacements only.
Do not change files or memory yet.
```

Save the accepted correction yourself; you can also ask the AI to update the summary, writing style, or current phase. Correcting memory does not automatically repair prose. If the plot changes, decide separately which files need revision.

## Record progress before a new chat

Record completed work, open decisions, and the next task. A new chat retains project memory, but it does not include every old message or the whole book in full. Open the target and quote key evidence before continuing.

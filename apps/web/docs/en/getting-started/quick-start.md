# Web writing quick start

Open or create your first work, draft your opening passage by hand or with the AI assistant, and save or export your manuscript.

## Step 1: Enter your first work

[Sign in](https://app.zenstory.ai/login) and open the [dashboard home](https://app.zenstory.ai/dashboard), then start in whichever way fits what you have ready:

1. **Choose a format and start writing**: Pick **Long-form Novel**, **Short Story**, or **Short-drama Script**, enter a brief idea (or click an example prompt below to fill it in), and click **Start Writing**. The workbench opens the work and sends your idea to the AI assistant automatically; leaving the box blank and clicking **Start Writing** opens a clean workspace. On a new account, you can also click **Open it** directly on the **Your first work is ready** card at the top.
2. **Import an existing draft**: If you already have a `.txt` or `.md` manuscript, select its format and click **Have a draft already? Import it (txt / md)** below the creation box. ZenStory creates a project named after your file, splits recognizable chapter headings into individual chapters without calling the AI or using your daily AI messages, and opens Chapter 1.

## Step 2: Find files, the editor, and AI chat

Inside a project, the desktop workspace is divided into three panes (on phones, switch between the bottom **Files**, **Editor**, and **AI** tabs):

- **Left sidebar**: Manage outlines, characters, worldbuilding, drafts or scripts, and materials under **Files**, attach writing skills under **Skills**, or browse analyzed reference novels under **Materials**.
- **Center editor**: Write and review documents, with word and paragraph counts, **History**, **Humanize**, and save status along the bottom bar.
- **Right AI assistant**: Brainstorm settings, generate outlines, or draft and revise chapters. Use **AI Memory** or **New chat** in the header, and switch between **Fast** and **Quality** modes at the bottom of the input box.

For the full top toolbar and settings reference, see [Interface Overview](../user-guide/interface-overview.md).

## Step 3: Write your first passage

You can either let the AI draft an opening or write directly in the editor:

- **Draft with the AI assistant**: In an empty editor, click **Let AI write the opening**, or type your request in the AI chat and press `Enter` to send (`Shift + Enter` adds a line break). When the AI creates or updates files, its reply lists each file as a card—click a file card to open it in the editor, or click **Undo** on the card to revert that file edit.
- **Create and write files yourself**: Click **New Chapter** (or **New Script** in screenplay projects) or **New Outline** in the empty editor, or click `+` next to a folder in **Files**. The editor auto-saves 3 seconds after you stop typing, or you can press `Ctrl/Cmd + S` or click **Save** at any time. Saved body changes are recorded in **History**; consecutive saves within a short window may be merged into a single version.

## Step 4: Revise passages and export a backup

1. **Revise a selected passage**: Highlight text in the editor and click **Quote** (`Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Q`) to attach those lines above the chat input with your instructions, or click **Humanize** (`Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + R`) in the bottom status bar, review the split diff, and click **Finish Review**.
2. **Export to `.txt`**: Click **Export whole work (TXT)** (or **Export whole script (TXT)**) in the top bar to download all chapters merged in order as a UTF-8 `.txt` file, or open **More Actions → Download current file (TXT)** to download only the active file.

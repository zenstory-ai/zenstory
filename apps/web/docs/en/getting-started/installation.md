# Account registration and login

The ZenStory writing workbench runs directly in your browser at [app.zenstory.ai](https://app.zenstory.ai) with nothing to install.

## 1. Browser and network requirements

ZenStory works on current desktop and mobile versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox. Your projects are stored in the cloud, and AI streaming and auto-save require an active internet connection. Keep browser `localStorage` enabled so the editor can protect unsaved drafts locally if a tab closes or reloads.

## 2. Create an account

1. Open the [registration page](https://app.zenstory.ai/register) (or click **Sign up** on the login page).
2. Enter your **Email** (type `@` to pick from common email domain suggestions) and **Password** (at least 6 characters and up to 72 bytes; click the eye icon to show or hide the password).
3. When referral invites are enabled, you can click **Have an invite code?** to enter an optional code.
4. Click **Create account**. If the email is already registered, inline buttons let you **Sign in** or **Verify email** directly.

## 3. Verify your email and choose what you write

### 1. Enter the 6-digit verification code

After you submit the registration form, ZenStory sends a 6-digit verification code to your email (valid for 5 minutes) and opens **Verify your email**:

- Type or paste the 6-digit code and click **Verify email**.
- If the email has not arrived after a minute, check your spam or promotions folder, then click **Resend verification code** (available every 60 seconds).
- **Mistyped your email address?** Click **Correct email** on the verification screen, enter your **New email** and **Registration password**, and click **Update and send code** to fix the address and send a fresh code right away.

### 2. Pick what you want to write

Once your email is verified, ZenStory asks **What do you want to write?**—choose **Long-form Novel**, **Short Story**, or **Short-drama Script** (or click **Skip for now** in the top right). ZenStory prepares your first work automatically and takes you to the dashboard home.

## 4. Sign in and reset your password

### 1. Sign in

On the [login page](https://app.zenstory.ai/login), enter your **Email or username** and **Password**, then click **Sign in**. If your email has not been verified yet, ZenStory takes you to the verification screen and sends a fresh code.

### 2. Reset a forgotten password

1. Click [Forgot password?](https://app.zenstory.ai/forgot-password) above the password field on the login page.
2. Enter your registered email address and click **Send verification code**.
3. Check your inbox for the 6-digit reset code (valid for 10 minutes), enter the **Verification code**, **New password** (at least 6 characters, up to 72 bytes), and **Confirm new password**, and click **Reset password**.
4. Sign in with your new password. If you still cannot access your account, email [support@zenstory.ai](mailto:support@zenstory.ai) from your registered address.

## 5. Profile, appearance, and sign-out

Click the gear icon in the top right to open **Settings**:

- **Profile**: View your username and registered email, or click **Log out**.
- **General**: Switch the interface between **简体中文** and **English**, choose **Dark Theme** or **Light Theme**, pick from 6 accent colors, or toggle usage analytics for the current browser.
- **Log out**: You can also click your avatar in the top right to open the user menu and choose **Log out**.

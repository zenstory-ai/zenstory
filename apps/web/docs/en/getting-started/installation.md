# Account registration and login

Register and sign in at [app.zenstory.ai](https://app.zenstory.ai). No installation is required.

## Register

1. Open [Register](https://app.zenstory.ai/register) and enter a username, email, password, and password confirmation.
2. Use at least 3 characters for the username and 6 for the password. Both passwords must match.
3. If the page shows an invitation code, follow its hint. Leave it empty when marked optional.
4. Read and accept the terms and privacy policy, then submit.
5. Enter the emailed verification code and follow the onboarding steps into the workbench.

If the email is missing, check its spelling and your spam folder. Wait for the resend countdown before requesting another code.

## Sign in

Enter your username or email and password on [Login](https://app.zenstory.ai/login). If Google login is offered, follow its authorization prompts.

An account created through Google may not have an email password yet. If Google is unavailable, use **Forgot Password** to set one, or contact support.

Follow the redirect to the workbench or recent project. On a shared device, sign out through the user menu when finished.

## Reset your password

1. Open [Forgot Password](https://app.zenstory.ai/forgot-password) from Login.
2. Enter your registered email to request a code.
3. Within 10 minutes, enter the six-digit code, a new password, and its confirmation. Submit, then sign in with the new password.

Wait for the countdown before resending. If online reset is unavailable, contact [support@zenstory.ai](mailto:support@zenstory.ai) from your registered email. Do not share passwords or verification codes.
